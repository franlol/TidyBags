# TidyBags

All your bags in one clean window, for WoW Forever.


- One grid for every bag, reagent bag and keyring apart or together
- Search, sorting, item level, quality borders, grey junk
- Your other characters' bags, and their item counts in tooltips
- Two looks: Flaty and Blizzard

Open the options with `/tb` or the minimap icon.
