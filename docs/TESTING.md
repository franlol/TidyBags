# In-game checklist

## Opening
- [ ] B, the backpack button and each bag button open and close the window; any bag button closes it when open (also a different bag than the one that opened it), and B works after it
- [ ] Escape closes it; the close button closes it
- [ ] Opens by itself at a vendor and at a mailbox, closes when leaving
- [ ] No Blizzard bag frame shows at any time

## Items
- [ ] Right click uses, equips and sells; Shift-click splits; drag and drop works
- [ ] Ctrl-click dresses up, Shift-click links in chat
- [ ] Items used in combat work, no "blocked action" (`/console taintLog 1`)
- [ ] New items glow until hovered (they keep glowing after closing; the reagent bag's stop when the bags close, the game does it); quest items show their mark; cooldowns spin
- [ ] Junk shows the coin at a vendor and is grey when the option is on
- [ ] Item level on equipment; quality border on and off, both looks
- [ ] Search greys what does not match; cleared when the window closes

## Bags
- [ ] Reagent bag: apart (title + free count) or with the rest
- [ ] Keyring (once the character has a key): apart or with the rest; rows of 4 that grow as it fills; its slot in the bag slots row takes a key from the cursor; the keyring button of the bag bar opens the window
- [ ] No authenticator: 4 locked slots at the end and the add-slots button (its popup)
- [ ] Bag slots row: hover dims the other bags' items with the search dimming, marks and glows covered too, cooldowns faded (search state comes back on leave); an outline (accent / gold) wraps the hovered bag's slots, following rows; drag a bag out and in;
      right click shows the game's bag options
- [ ] Sort button sorts; right click shows the cleanup options
- [ ] Bag slots row: same size and spacing as the item slots; shown bags: accent border (Flaty) or the bag bar's open-bag highlight (Blizzard); click a bag (backpack, bags, reagent bag, keyring) hides its slots and dims it like a search miss; click again shows them; the free count follows; an item on the cursor still goes into the bag; old "Hidden" reagent/keyring settings show as unchecked bags

## Characters
- [ ] Another character appears after it logs in once
- [ ] Its bags show read only, with tooltips; search filters them
- [ ] Tooltip counts per character and total; off in options
- [ ] Forgetting a character in options removes it

## Options
- [ ] Enabled on/off with the bags open: they stay open in the other look; closed: they stay closed
- [ ] Enabled off: B opens Blizzard's own bags (they update, sort, search), no tooltip counts, other options greyed; on again: TidyBags
- [ ] Every change shows at once on the open bags, both looks
- [ ] Columns, item size, spacing, scale; lock position; reset position
- [ ] The window's top stays put when it grows or shrinks (bag toggles, item size, columns); dragging keeps the new place after a reload
- [ ] Texts: count and item level font, size, outline, position and offsets; they keep their size on screen when the item size changes; Reset
- [ ] Profiles: new, copy, reset, delete; language switch rebuilds the window
